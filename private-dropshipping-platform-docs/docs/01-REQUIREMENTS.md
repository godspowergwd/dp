# Requirements

## Functional requirements
### Product research
Store product URL/source, images, cost, shipping information, supplier, research notes, competitor observations, demand signals and a configurable opportunity score.

### Product management
Statuses: discovered, researching, shortlisted, approved, rejected, drafting, published, paused, archived.

### AI Studio
Generate titles, descriptions, benefits, FAQs, ad copy, image concepts, scripts and structured research summaries.

### Suppliers
Store supplier identity, source URLs/API IDs, costs, variants, fulfillment notes and reliability observations.

### Store integrations
Support connected ecommerce stores through adapter interfaces.

### Orders
Import/sync orders where permitted, store status transitions, fulfillment references and tracking information.

### Analytics
Track revenue, product cost, estimated fees, ad spend when available, gross margin and AI/API costs.

## Non-functional requirements
Security, auditability, idempotent integrations, retries, rate limiting, backups, responsive UI and structured logs.
