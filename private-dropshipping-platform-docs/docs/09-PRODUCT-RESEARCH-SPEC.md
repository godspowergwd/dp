# Product Research Specification

## Inputs
Product URL/source, supplier data, images, cost and operator notes.

## Research output
- problem solved
- target audience
- differentiation
- demand signals
- competitor observations
- likely objections
- estimated margin
- operational risks
- recommended decision

## Scoring
Use configurable weighted scores rather than claiming an AI score is objective:
demand, margin, competition, shipping, creative potential, supplier confidence and risk.

## Output
AI produces structured JSON plus a human-readable report.
